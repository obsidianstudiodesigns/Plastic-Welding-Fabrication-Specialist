The right weld for every joint

HDPE and polypropylene pipe, fittings and sheet, joined with the method the job calls for.

  1. Pipe ends are faced square, heated on a hot plate and pressed together under controlled pressure. The double bead it leaves is there for you to inspect.

  2. Couplers and fittings with heating coils built in, fused by a control unit. The go-to for trenches, tie-ins and tight spaces where a machine won't fit.

  3. A hand extruder lays a bead of molten plastic along a seam. Used to build manholes, tanks, sumps and fittings from HDPE and PP sheet and pipe.

  4. Small-bore pipe and socket fittings are heated on matching bushes and pushed together. Quick, strong joints for service lines and smaller fittings.
