What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer enquiries, quote and do the work, such as:

  • your name, company, cellphone number and email address (WhatsApp shares your number with us when you message us)
  • job details: the site location, dates, pipe sizes and materials, drawings, photos and scope you send
  • site contact details and access arrangements
  • job offers, contracts, timesheets, quotes, invoices and payment records

Why we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry or job offer and prepare a quote or rate
  • to plan, carry out and complete welding, fabrication and installation work
  • to meet site safety, induction and access requirements you or the main contractor set
  • to manage payments and keep the records the law requires

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we will not add you to a marketing list.

Who we share it with

We only share information when we need to: with the main contractor or site management on a job you are part of, with suppliers where needed for your order, or when the law requires it. WhatsApp (Meta) and Gmail (Google) carry our messages under their own privacy policies.

Photos of work

We may share photos of welds, fabrications and installations we have done. We focus on the work, not on people, and we do not share photos from sites where the client or contractor has asked us not to. If you would like a photo removed, tell us and we will take it down.

This website, cookies and third parties

This website has no accounts and no database. It does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The enquiry form does not send anything to us or store anything: it only opens WhatsApp on your device with your message filled in, and nothing is sent unless you press send in WhatsApp yourself. The WhatsApp, phone, email and map links work the same way.

To load the website, your browser fetches files from these providers, which may record your IP address and browser details in their standard server logs:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces
  • jsDelivr, which supplies the icons

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and job details for as long as we need them to do the work and answer questions afterwards. Financial records are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually five years, and then deleted.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep information safe, including keeping it on password-protected devices and accounts and only sharing it when needed.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or delete it
  • object to us using it, or withdraw consent you have given

Contact us on the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
